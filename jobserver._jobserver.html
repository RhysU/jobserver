def _callback_wrapper(seqno: int, fn, /, *args, **kwargs): ¶

Call fn(*args, **kwargs) wrapping any Exception in CallbackRaised.

The seqno is the when_done(...) sequence number from registering fn and is stamped on any CallbackRaised raised here. A non-Exception BaseException is not wrapped and propagates raw.
