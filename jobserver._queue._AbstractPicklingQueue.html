jobserver._queue._AbstractPicklingQueue

class documentation

class _AbstractPicklingQueue(_AbstractQueue[T]):

Known subclasses: jobserver._queue.MPMCQueue, jobserver._queue.SPSCQueue

Constructor: _AbstractPicklingQueue(source)

View In Hierarchy

_AbstractQueue whose get()/put() pickle generic objects.

Objects are serialized/deserialized with ForkingPickler and moved across the pipe as length-prefixed byte frames. The read/write guards keep those multi-syscall frames intact under concurrent access.
